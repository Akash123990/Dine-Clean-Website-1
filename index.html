Frequently asked questions

Simple answers, before you get in touch.

How can I place an enquiry?

Add products to your enquiry list, then send it directly on WhatsApp. You can also contact us by phone or email.

Can I ask about more than one product?

Yes. Add every product you are interested in to the enquiry list and send one clear message to DineClean.

Can I discuss a bulk requirement?

Yes. Share the products and quantity you are looking for through WhatsApp, phone, or email to discuss availability.

Where can I get product and pack information?

Use the quick view on any product, then contact us for the latest pack options, availability, and price information.

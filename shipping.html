Shipping Information

How delivery and dispatch details are confirmed for DineClean product orders. Last updated: July 20, 2026.

Order confirmation comes first

DineClean confirms product availability, quantity, delivery location, and any applicable delivery details directly with you before an order is finalized.

Delivery availability

Delivery or dispatch availability may depend on the products requested, order quantity, and delivery location. Please do not assume availability until DineClean confirms the details of your enquiry.

Timing and charges

Any estimated dispatch timing, delivery method, and applicable charges will be discussed at the time of order confirmation. These details may vary based on the specific requirement.

Receiving your order

When you receive an order, please check the products and packaging promptly. Contact DineClean as soon as possible if you have a question or notice an issue.

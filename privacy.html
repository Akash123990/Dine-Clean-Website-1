Privacy Policy

How DineClean handles details shared through this website and direct enquiries. Last updated: July 20, 2026.

What we collect

When you contact DineClean through the enquiry form, email, phone, or WhatsApp, you may choose to share your name, phone number, email address, product interests, and message details.

How we use your details

We use the details you share to respond to your product enquiry, discuss availability or product information, and provide relevant customer support. We do not use this website to collect payment details.

Browser storage

The website may store a wishlist and product enquiry list locally in your browser so those selections remain available while you browse. This information stays on your device and is not sent to DineClean unless you choose to send an enquiry through WhatsApp.

Third-party services

When you choose to use WhatsApp or your email application, those services handle the message according to their own privacy policies. Please review their policies if you have questions about how they process information.

Your choices

You can contact DineClean to ask about the personal information you have shared or to request an update to it. We will respond through the contact details below.
